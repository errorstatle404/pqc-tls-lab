# Sample run

Generated by `scripts/run-bench.sh` on a Windows 11 Pro laptop (WSL2 + Docker Desktop, x86_64), loopback network between the two containers, N=200 handshakes per scenario, OpenSSL 3.5.4 + nginx 1.28.0.

Your timings will differ. Byte counts should match to within a couple of bytes: ML-KEM and ML-DSA sizes are fixed, but an ECDSA signature's DER encoding varies by 1–2 bytes from one certificate to the next.

## Reading it

- **Hybrid costs little.** Scenario 2 vs 1: +1,232 B client→server, +1,089 B server→client, +0.42 ms median.
- **The silent downgrade.** Scenario 3 negotiates classical `x25519` on the "hybrid" endpoint. Scenario 4 shows the strict endpoint forcing the hybrid via HelloRetryRequest, for +0.37 ms over scenario 2 on loopback (one extra round trip on a real network).
- **PQ signatures dominate.** Scenario 6 sends 16,415 B server→client, 11.8× the classical 1,389 B, and has the highest median (4.28 ms) and p95 (8.33 ms).
- **ML-KEM-768 encapsulates 3.7× faster than X25519; ML-DSA-65 signs 26× slower than ECDSA P-256** on this machine.
